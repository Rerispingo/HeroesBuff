# HeroesBuff
This mod improves the core mechanics of each hero (Sauda, Quincy and Benjamin only for now).

## Quincy Buffs :
- Range increased by 50% up to level 14
- Range increased by 75% starting at level 15
- Damage increased by 1 at Level 4
- Damage increased by 2 at Level 15
- Pierce increased by 1 at Level 2
- Pierce increased by 2 at Level 15
- Quincy can pop Lead and Frozen Bloons at Level 5
### Rapid Shot Ability:
- Cooldown reduced by 33%
### Storm of Arrows Ability:
- Damage increased by 150%

## Sauda Buffs : 
- Range increased by 20% on all levels
- Damage increased by 2 at Level 10
- Damage increased by 2 at Level 20
- Pierce increased by 1 on all levels
- Attack speed increased by 10% * level (Ex: 50% at level 5)
### Leaping Sword Ability Buffs :
- Cooldown reduced by 50%
- Impact damage increased by 400%
- Continuous damage increased by 100%
- Ability duration increased by 50%

## Benjamin Buffs :
- Cost reduced by 400
- Cash per round increased by 150%
